    {Computational McCabe--Thiele Distillation Simulator}
    {Self-directed project, Unit Operations}
    {MATLAB, JavaScript}{}
    \cvBulletsStart
      \item Developed a modular implementation of the \textbf{McCabe--Thiele method} for multi-section distillation columns with arbitrary feeds, side streams, and subcooled reflux.
      \item Implemented section-wise material balances, $q$-lines and operating lines, vapour--liquid equilibrium fitting and interpolation, curve-intersection detection, Murphree efficiency with pseudo-equilibrium correction, and automated stage stepping.
      \item Structured the solver as reusable functions behind a single execution script so it applies across a range of Unit Operations problems, then ported the validated model to a browser-based \textbf{JavaScript/HTML} implementation.
    \cvBulletsEnd

  \cvProject
    {Coupled Transport Modelling of Evaporative Cooling}
    {Mass Transfer course project}
    {COMSOL Multiphysics}{}
    \cvBulletsStart
      \item Built a \textbf{three-dimensional COMSOL model} coupling turbulent airflow, heat transfer, and evaporative mass transfer for cooling of a liquid-filled ceramic vessel.
      \item Solved a stationary turbulent-flow field followed by transient heat and moisture transport, then investigated the effects of \textbf{ambient temperature, relative humidity, and latent heat of vaporisation}.
      \item Quantified the contribution of evaporation: neglecting latent heat shifted the predicted 10-minute liquid temperature by roughly \textbf{14\%}, while relative humidity produced a substantially smaller effect.
    \cvBulletsEnd

  \cvProject
    {Nonlinear Material-Balance Modelling of Crystallisation}
    {Numerical Methods course project}
    {MATLAB}{Grade: 20 / 20}
    \cvBulletsStart
      \item Formulated a \textbf{six-variable nonlinear material-balance model} for purification by crystallisation and implemented a Newton-based solution scheme.
      \item Investigated the convergence limitations of the nonlinear system and, once the Newton formulation failed to converge, recovered candidate solutions through symbolic solution instead.
      \item Validated the result independently by reformulating the balances around different process boundaries and running component-level conservation checks.
    \cvBulletsEnd

  \cvProject
    {Biological Sorption: Biosorption and Bioaccumulation}
    {Introduction to Biological Sciences}
    {Literature review}{Grade: 20 / 20}
    \cvBulletsStart
      \item Surveyed the literature on \textbf{biosorption and bioaccumulation}, connecting biological sorption mechanisms to the adsorption and separation framework used in chemical engineering.
      \item Examined adsorption equilibria, isotherm modelling, batch material balances, and the roles of equilibrium and kinetics in adsorption dynamics.
    \cvBulletsEnd
